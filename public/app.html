📲 INSTALL ON ANY PHONE
Works on Android & iPhone. No App Store. No Play Store. Install directly.
How To Install Manually
📱 ANDROID (Chrome)
1. Open Chrome browser → go to iconik-global.web.app
2. Tap the 3-dot menu ⋮ at top right
3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add" — ICONIK appears on your home screen!
🍎 IPHONE (Safari)
1. Open Safari → go to iconik-global.web.app
2. Tap the Share button 📤 at the bottom
3. Scroll down → tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add" — ICONIK is now on your iPhone!
