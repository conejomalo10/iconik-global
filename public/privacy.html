1. Introduction

ICONIK Global ("ICONIK", "we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data. By using ICONIK, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

2. Data We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, country, city, and community preferences.

Profile Data: Profile photo, bio, interests, VIP tier, and any content you post on the platform.

Payment Information: Payment transactions are processed by third-party providers (Paystack, PayPal, cryptocurrency gateways). We do not store full card details on our servers.

Usage Data: We collect data on how you interact with ICONIK, including pages visited, features used, device type, browser, IP address, and approximate location.

Communications: Messages, comments, and other content you submit on the platform.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To personalize your experience and community recommendations
  • To send you platform notifications, updates, and marketing (you may opt out)
  • To ensure platform security and prevent fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To analyze platform performance and improve our services

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with:

  • Service Providers: Firebase (Google), Cloudinary, Paystack, PayPal — who process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect the safety of users
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger or acquisition, your data may be transferred to the successor entity

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you

  • Object to or restrict certain processing
