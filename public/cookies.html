What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences, keep you logged in, and understand how you use the site.

How ICONIK Uses Cookies

ICONIK uses the following types of cookies and similar technologies:

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the platform to function. They include Firebase Authentication session tokens that keep you logged in. Without these, ICONIK cannot work properly.

Preference Cookies

These remember your settings, such as your preferred community, theme preferences, and notification settings.

Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools to understand how members use ICONIK — which features are most popular, how long sessions last, and where users encounter difficulties. This data is anonymized and used only to improve the platform.

Third-Party Cookies

Some ICONIK features rely on third-party services that may set their own cookies:

  • Firebase/Google: Authentication and database services
  • Cloudinary: Media delivery
  • YouTube: Embedded video content (ICONIK TV)
  • Paystack/PayPal: Payment processing

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • View and delete cookies
  • Block all cookies or specific cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites

Note: Blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging into ICONIK.
