💳 Membership Subscriptions
Monthly recurring revenue from VIP Bronze ($5), VIP Silver ($12), and VIP Gold ($25) memberships across all 13 communities worldwide.

Coaches, clinics, and brands pay for the "ICONIK Verified Partner" badge — a premium listing that generates recurring monthly income.
🤝 Affiliate Commissions
3–20% commissions from Amazon Associates, iHerb, MyProtein, Gymshark, Udemy, Coursera and more. Tracked per click and purchase.
🎫 Events & Tickets
Fan events, meet & greets, virtual concerts. Ticketing integration with fan communities per category.
🏪 Marketplace
Fan merchandise, celebrity-endorsed products, wellness supplements. Listed products earn ICONIK a platform commission.
📢 Creator Fund & Sponsored Content
Top creators earn from the ICONIK Creator Fund. Brands pay to sponsor community posts, newsletters, and celebrity features.
Wellness Safeguards
